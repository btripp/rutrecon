Purchases

RutRecon Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. To cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Accuracy

RutRecon is a planning tool. Rut dates come from state wildlife agencies where they publish them and are labeled as estimates where they do not. Season dates are statewide summaries; always check your state's regulations before you hunt.
