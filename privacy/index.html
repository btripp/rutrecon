RUTRECON

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

RutRecon has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your phone

Saved spots, your hunting style, your settings and cached forecasts are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to our servers; we have none. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app sends to work

  • A spot's coordinates go to Open-Meteo for the weather forecast, past weather, temperature normals and the local time zone, and to the FCC Census Area API to find the county.
  • Town names you type in Add spot go to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find the town.

These providers receive the request and your IP address as part of normal internet traffic, under their own policies: Open-Meteo, FCC.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location only when you tap Use GPS, to fill in a new spot. It is not tracked in the background or sent anywhere except as the spot coordinates described above.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts to confirm what you bought. We never see your payment details.

Sharing and ratings

Share card opens your phone's share sheet with text you can review before sending. The rating prompt is the store's own; we see only the public rating if you leave one.

Feedback

Send feedback opens your own email app with the app version, phone model and your spot's county filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. Your spot names and coordinates are never included.

Children

RutRecon is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
